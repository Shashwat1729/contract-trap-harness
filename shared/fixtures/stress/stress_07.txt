MASTER   SUBSCRIPTION  AGREEMENT

This  Master  Subscription  Agreement  is  entered  into  between
Larkspur   Analytics,   Inc.   ("Company")   and   the   customer
identified  on  the  Order  Form  ("Customer").

1.1  Definitions.   Capitalized  terms  used  herein  have  the
meanings  set  forth  in  the  Order  Form  or  as  defined  be-
low.

1.2  Term.  The  Initial  Term  is  twelve  (12)  months.  There-
after,   this  Agreement  renews  for  successive  renewal
terms  of  twelve  (12)  months  each,  unless  either  party
provides  written  notice  of  non-renewal  at  least  ninety
(90)  days  before  the  end  of  the  then-current  term.

1.3  Fees.   Customer  shall  pay  the  fees  set  forth  in  the
Order  Form  within  thirty  (30)  days  of  invoice.

1.4  Limitation  of  Liability.   Except  for  breaches  of  con-
fidentiality  or  gross  negligence,  each  party's  total  lia-
bility  under  this  Agreement  shall  not  exceed  the  fees
paid  by  Customer  in  the  twelve  (12)  months  preceding
the  claim.

1.5  Audit  Rights.   Company  may  audit  Customer's  use  of
the  Service  once  per  year  on  thirty  (30)  days  prior
written  notice.

1.6  Governing  Law.   This  Agreement  is  governed  by  the
laws  of  the  State  of  Delaware.

1.7  Non-Disparagement.   Neither  party  shall  disparage
the  other  party,  whether  during  the  Term  or  after  its
termination.

1.8  Miscellaneous.   This  Agreement  constitutes  the  en-
tire  agreement  between  the  parties  regarding  its  sub-
ject  matter.
