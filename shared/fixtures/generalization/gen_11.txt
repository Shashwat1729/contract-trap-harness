MANAGED SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Copperfield Systems Group ("Vendor") and Customer.

Section 21 -- Post-Termination Services

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, Vendor will permanently erase all Customer Data from its production systems within fifteen (15) days. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Vendor will continue to hold a backup copy of Customer Data for up to six (6) months following termination in order to support Customer's transition to a replacement provider, and will make that backup available to Customer upon request during that window.

Section 22 -- Return of Property

Each party will return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information within thirty (30) days of termination.
