SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Harbor Ledger Systems, LLC ("Vendor") and the entity signing the cover page ("Customer").

Section 6 -- Term

This Agreement has an initial term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date and will not automatically extend beyond that term without both parties' written agreement to a new term.

Section 7 -- Notice of Non-Renewal

If either party elects to extend under a mutually agreed new term, the electing party must give the other party at least twenty (20) days' notice before the then-current term expires, and the parties will negotiate the terms of the extension in good faith.

Section 8 -- Fees and Payment

Fees are invoiced quarterly in advance and are due within thirty (30) days of invoice.
