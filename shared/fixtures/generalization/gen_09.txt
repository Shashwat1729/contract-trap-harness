STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

Between Redwater Cloud Partners ("Vendor") and Customer.

Section 30 -- Non-Disparagement

Customer shall not make, publish, or encourage any statement, whether written or oral, that disparages Vendor, its products, or its personnel, during the Term and for two (2) years thereafter.

Section 31 -- Publicity

Neither party will issue a press release referencing the other without prior written consent.
