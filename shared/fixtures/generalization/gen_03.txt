CLOUD SERVICES ORDER AGREEMENT

Between Ferrous Data Corp. ("Vendor") and Customer.

Article 12 -- Limitation of Liability

Except for a breach of the confidentiality obligations in Article 15, Vendor's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited for any claim brought by Customer, regardless of the theory of liability asserted.

Article 13 -- Indemnification

Each party will indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its own gross negligence, subject to the process described in Article 14.

Article 14 -- Claims Process

A party seeking indemnification must provide prompt written notice of any claim.
