Between Vantage Alloy Systems ("Vendor") and Customer.

Section 16 -- Non-Compete

For twenty-four (24) months after this Agreement ends for any reason, Customer shall not engage, directly or indirectly, in any business that competes with Vendor anywhere in the world, whether as an owner, employee, consultant, or investor.

Section 17 -- Survival

The obligations in Sections 15 (Confidentiality) and 16 (Non-Compete) survive termination of this Agreement.
